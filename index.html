Prohet Orpheus

Orpheus was a legendary musician, poet, and prophet in ancient Greek mythology. He was known for his ability to charm all living things with his music and his journey to the underworld to retrieve his wife, Eurydice.

Orpheus was the son of the muse Calliope and the Thracian king Oeagrus. He was gifted with a lyre, which he played so beautifully that it could soothe wild beasts and even move inanimate objects. His music was said to be so powerful that it could influence the natural world and the gods themselves.
